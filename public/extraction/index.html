Extraction yield = beverage weight × TDS ÷ dose. It is a reading, not an estimate: without a refractometer there is nothing to compute. For filter, weigh the brewed coffee, not the water you poured. The bed holds back about twice the dose. Stir the cup, filter the sample, and let it reach room temperature before reading.

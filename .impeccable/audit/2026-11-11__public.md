Audit of `public/`: the launcher (`index.html`), cupping, espresso, filter and `shared/`.

**Evidence.** `impeccable detect` over the source (29 findings) and over each served page (3 findings). A Playwright probe on the four pages at 390×844 and 360×844, light and dark, with emulated mobile and touch. It measured text contrast against the composited ground, text under 11px, targets under 24 and 44px, unnamed controls, headings, landmarks and horizontal overflow. Each app was then driven into its working state: filter (coffee added, brew sheet open), espresso (coffee added, two shots logged) and cupping (lineup set, scoresheet open).

**Not tested:** 320px width, 200% text zoom, a physical device, screen-reader output and live/synced states. Touch drag was checked in code (pointer capture, `pointercancel`, `touch-action`), not with real gestures.

## Implementation Integrity Verdict

**Pass.** This is a system written for this product: the anchored scale, the two-face type split and a single data ink with a documented meaning. Every one of the 16 page/theme/width combinations measured zero text-contrast failures on its first screen. The drift that does exist is local, and DESIGN.md already names the rule each one breaks.

Detector results, checked in context:
- `gradient-text` on `index.html`: **false positive.** The source hit is a comment explaining that the gradient was removed. The rendered page has no element with `background-clip: text`.
- `nested-cards` on `#stepper-coffees` and `#stepper-cups`: **false positive.** These are stepper control groups, not cards.
- `design-system-font-size` hits on the 46px wordmark and the 5.4px and 7px wheel labels: **documented exemptions** (DESIGN.md › Typography, › Components).
- `monotonous-spacing` on `filter/index.html`: low signal. The inline markup is small, and the 4px repeats are list gaps.
- The 9px `.scale-num` ticks are the one documented sub-11px exemption.
- The cupping stepper "−" at 1.7:1 is disabled, so it is exempt from 1.4.3.

## Detailed Findings

**[P2] `.empty` means two things, and the empty-state rule centres number-field labels**
- Location: `shared/components.css:554` (`.empty { padding; text-align: center }`) collides with `.num-field.empty` (`:880`, set by the apps on an unset field).
- Category: Implementation Integrity / Responsive
- Impact: On the filter brew sheet, Coffee's label sits flush left and Water, Time and Grind are centred. That is a visible stagger down a column the reader scans. Espresso shares the component and is exposed the same way.
- Recommendation: Rename the empty-state block (for example `.empty-state`), or scope it, so the state class on a field inherits nothing from it.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] Filter readouts print "no value" in the data ink**
- Location: `filter/app.js:3199–3217`. These `readout-value` spans never get `.empty`. Espresso does this right at `espresso/app.js:4076–4084`.
- Category: Theming
- Impact: Ratio, drawdown and bloom show a slate "—" before anything is measured. DESIGN.md: "the data ink is for values a cupper actually set"; an unset slot is "an em dash in secondary ink".
- Recommendation: Add `.empty` when the value is null, matching espresso.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] Text below the 11px floor**
- Location: `.log-flag` 10px (`shared/components.css:380`); `.num-field.compact .num-label` and `.num-unit` 10px (`filter/styles.css:90,92`).
- Category: Accessibility
- Impact: These are labels with meaning of their own (pour labels, units, a flag on a shot). DESIGN.md: "No text below 11px anywhere a cupper needs to read it." They are read at arm's length in poor light.
- Recommendation: 11px.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] Type sizes have crept off the ramp again**
- Location: `shared/components.css` 12px (`:759`, `:957`, `:1513`), 14px (`:1355`, `:1402`, `:1446`), 19px (`:413`) and 26px (`:400`, `:424`); `filter/styles.css` 12px ×6 and 14px; `cupping/styles.css:2526` 12px.
- Category: Implementation Integrity
- Impact: This is the accumulation DESIGN.md's Typography section was written to stop ("12 and 14 had crept in between 11, 13 and 15"), now back in the shared layer and the filter app.
- Recommendation: Snap 12→11 or 13, 14→13 or 15, 19→20 and 26→24. The `16px` on `.field-input` (`:1321`, `:1341`) stops iOS zooming into a focused field. It is a legitimate exemption but not yet written down; add it to DESIGN.md beside the wordmark.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] The filter board has no page heading and no main landmark**
- Location: `filter/index.html`. The rendered board has no headings (`[]`) and no `<main>`. Espresso has `<main class="board-scroll">` and an sr-only `<h1 id="board-heading">` holding the coffee name.
- Category: Accessibility (WCAG 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks, 1.3.1)
- Impact: A screen-reader user has no way to jump to the log or to learn which coffee they are on. Sheets in espresso and on the launcher open with an `h3` straight under the `h1` (the detector's `skipped-heading`).
- Recommendation: Port espresso's `main` and sr-only `h1` to filter. Make sheet titles `h2`.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] A data-ink stripe down the advice card**
- Location: `.tip` / `.tip.sure` (`shared/components.css:936–941`), `.kit-fits` (`:1633`). The rendered espresso "Finding the window" card shows a slate rule down its left edge.
- Category: Implementation Integrity / Theming
- Impact: DESIGN.md says "No … accent stripe on the left edge of a card" and "If it is not a value, it is not the data ink". Here the slate means *confidence*, which is not a value. The code comment documents the choice, so this is a contradiction between the two documents rather than an accident.
- Recommendation: Decide which document is right. Either carry confidence in the words and weight (the comment already says it is "never by colour alone") and draw the rule in `--rail`, or amend DESIGN.md to grant this exemption the way it grants the CATA chips theirs.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable quieter`

**[P3] The separator dot between log fields reads at 2.37:1 (2.64 dark)**
- Location: `.log-dot { opacity: 0.6 }` (`shared/components.css:376`) on top of `--text-faint`.
- Category: Accessibility
- Impact: Punctuation only, so it fails no criterion, but it is the one sub-3:1 glyph on the board.
- Recommendation: Drop the opacity and keep `--text-faint`.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P3] The anchored scale is implemented three times**
- Location: `cupping/app.js` (around `:4000`), `espresso/app.js:2869`, `filter/app.js:1996`. Each has its own pointer, keyboard and settle code.
- Category: Implementation Integrity
- Impact: All three are correct today (`role="slider"`, arrow keys, `pointercancel`). The "one signature component" can still drift three ways the next time one is fixed.
- Recommendation: Move the espresso and filter copies into `shared/` the way the wheel was. Cupping keeping its own is a recorded decision.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P3] Unminified JS**
- Location: `cupping/app.js` 276 KB, `espresso/app.js` 296 KB, `filter/app.js` 212 KB.
- Category: Performance
- Impact: The service worker pays this cost once, but the first visit on a weak connection is the acquisition moment PRODUCT.md cares about.
- Recommendation: Weigh this when the open stack decision is taken. On its own it does not justify adding a build step.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

- **The shared layer is where the drift collects now.** Every off-ramp size, the sub-11px text and the class collision are in `shared/components.css` or `filter/styles.css`. Cupping, the most-audited surface, has one stray 12px.
- **The filter app trails espresso on rules the two already share** (`.empty` readouts, `main` and `h1`). It is the one instrument PRODUCT.md says has been tested only by its author.

## Positive Findings

- **Zero text-contrast failures** on every first screen, both themes, both widths. The four-ground worst-case table in DESIGN.md holds up in the browser.
- Every interactive element is ≥44px apart from the documented `.help-btn` (19px drawn, 45px hit area via `::after`). The scales handle `pointercancel`, and the root sets `touch-action: manipulation`.
- Every removed outline has a replacement: `:focus-within` on number fields, a dashed stroke ring on wheel wedges, and border changes on text fields.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` rules exist on every page. The keyframes match DESIGN.md's budget of four (plus one `arrive` in the shared layer worth checking against that list).
- No `user-scalable=no` anywhere. Fonts are self-hosted, the landing page preloads them, and no runtime dependencies are pulled from a CDN.
